Help

  • Comment, and talk about it with the agentUse the comment tool, to the left of the pen: click a slide to comment on that spot, or drag to comment on an area. With any tool, right-clicking a spot does the same, and dragging with the right button picks an area. Write in the bubble that opens there and press “Add”. Click a numbered marker to read its thread in a bubble. The agent can start threads of its own (they are marked as the agent’s and in another color) and reply to yours. The comments button at the top right opens a panel with every thread on the slide: click a thread to select it. When the agent started the thread or has answered in it, a box opens to reply; your own comment is changed right in its text. Press “Resolve” when a thread is settled (resolved threads are folded away and can be opened again). While you point at a thread, its place on the slide blinks; a thread about the whole slide shades the whole slide. That panel is also where you comment on the whole slide: press the button at its bottom and a box opens there. Drag the left edge of the panel to change its width.
  • Draw, then tell the agent in chatDraw with the pen or highlighter (hold Shift for a straight line, Ctrl for a rectangle), then send your instructions in the chat. The agent reads your marks and fixes the deck.
  • Submit your reviewWhen you are done, press “Submit review” at the top right. It is sent to the agent at once, with no confirmation: the agent is asked to read your marks, comments and new slides and to make the changes. The button works when there is something new for the agent to read, and its tooltip says what will be sent.
  • Add slidesIn the slide list below, hover between two slides to show “+”. Click it to put a blank new slide there. A new slide does not go into the PowerPoint file until the agent turns it into a real slide.
  • Reorder and deleteDrag slides in the list to reorder them. Right-click a slide in the list for a menu to add a new slide after it, move it, or delete it. Deleting happens right away, and the Undo button (Ctrl+Z) brings the slide back. Moving and deleting change the PowerPoint file; the file as it was before is kept as a backup. Drag the top edge of the list to change its height.
  • Start from a blank pageYou don’t need a PowerPoint file. Use “Open a deck” to start a blank sketchbook.
